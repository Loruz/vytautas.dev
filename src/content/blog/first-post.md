# Hello 👋

Finally — my first (and hopefully not the last) blog post on this newly re-created portfolio website.

For a long time, I’ve had this idea in the back of my mind that one day I would start writing and sharing my thoughts. But somehow, I never felt a strong enough pull or energy to actually begin. Maybe I didn’t have enough ideas. Maybe I didn’t see enough meaning in it at the time.

Lately though, the thought kept coming back: _it’s time to start_.  
And interestingly, the ideas themselves began forming more clearly in my head.

I also realized that writing about what you do — or what you’ve done — is a powerful way to re-analyze yourself. It forces reflection. It helps you see your own growth more objectively.

---

## The Old Website

About six years ago, I had a similar wave of inspiration. I saw a blog post from someone I was following online. He had just started writing himself. I remember thinking: _If he’s doing it, why shouldn’t I?_

But apparently, it just wasn’t the right time.

Back then, I chose a somewhat unusual meta-framework called **Gridsome**. I had read somewhere that it was a solid tool for static-generated websites. At that time, **Nuxt.js** wasn’t as popular as it is today, and I was working much more with **Vue.js** than I do now with React.

A few `npm install` commands later — and I had a working website.

The best part? Everything was built manually. No shortcuts. No heavy abstractions.

Eventually, I commented out the `/blog` section, telling myself I’d come back to it someday.

I never did. 😄

This how it looked like for straight 6 years:
![Old website](/blog-images/old-vytautas.dev.png)

## The New Website

About a month ago, I decided it was time to revisit this idea — properly this time.

For this version, I chose **Astro**. It felt fresh, lightweight, and significantly less complex for the kind of website I wanted to build.

Here, I plan to write not only general thoughts and reflections but also technical retrospectives about my side projects — some of which have become real, living products, and others that are very close to getting there.

I’ll also share ideas currently on my mind — whether that’s tech, AI, chess, or any other topics that genuinely interest me.

So, happy reading.

First of all — for myself.  
But hopefully for you as well. 🚀
